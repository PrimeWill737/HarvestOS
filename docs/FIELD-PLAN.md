# Field validation and commercial roadmap

Start with one Plateau tomato cluster. Prove records, explainable decisions and follow-through before expansion. The product is coordination infrastructure, not a claim that marketplaces or storage are novel.

## Release gates

Hackathon: one authorized harvest-to-buyer journey, with simulated data labeled. Field V1: verified users/providers, actual logistics/storage, incidents/outbox and audited pilot transactions. Regional V2: analytics/optimization, multilingual access and financial integrations only after demonstrated value.

## Ten-week rollout

Week 1: interviews, PRD, role journeys, wireframes, ERD and backlog. Week 2: identity, schema, RBAC and interface foundation. Weeks 3–4: farms, harvests, demand, offers, inventory and orders. Week 5: urgency, matching and interventions. Weeks 6–7: transport, storage, incidents and fulfillment. Week 8: administration, reports, notification outbox and audit. Weeks 9–10: security/performance, restore rehearsal, training and field release candidate.

## 48-hour sequence and demo

0–8 hours: validated tomato problem, schema and shell. 8–20: forms and transactions. 20–32: urgency, cost comparison and operations. 32–40: low demand, delays, constraints, concurrency and usability. 40–48: stabilize, disclose simulations and rehearse.

Three-minute demo: measured farmer lot; compatible verified demand; factors/missing costs; partial accepted offer; competing allocation denied; receipt and independent review; reconciled remaining inventory. Explain that a sale is not automatically avoided loss. Use isolated fictional test identities or the read-only preview.

## Interviews

Proposed sample: at least five farmers, three produce traders, two bulk buyers and two transport/storage operators. Ask about a specific recent harvest:

1. Crop, original quantity, measured mass, sold/spoiled/held portions?
2. When was the buyer confirmed and how was price agreed?
3. Actual transport, storage, handling and transaction costs?
4. Why did pickup or payment fail?
5. What information would improve planning?
6. Would an authorized agent be more trusted/accessible than self-service?

## Pilot and metrics

Hypothesis: 10–20 farmers, a handful of buyers and a few verified providers in one cluster. These are targets, not committed participants. Seek at least 10 independently verified transactions, at least 80% onboarding completion with reasonable assistance, full reconciliation in tested stock cases and zero unexplained over-allocation.

Measure actual sold/lost/rejected/held mass by crop; net farmer revenue per verified kg with real costs; supply/demand coverage; time to buyer; pickup and order fulfillment; incidents/disputes; and operating costs. Log actual staff/provider/cloud costs outside the transaction ledger and assess cost per completed order. Track user failures and repeated workflow friction. Unknown prices, costs or weights remain unknown.

Avoided loss requires a credible baseline/comparison method and measured evidence. Do not derive it from a score, recommendation, reservation or sale. Publish study methods for research review.

## Revenue hypotheses

Explore modest fees on verified completed transactions only when user economics support them; recurring buyer subscriptions; cooperative aggregation plans; documented logistics coordination fees; and lawfully anonymized enterprise analytics. Do not charge vulnerable participants just to publish before value is established. This release does not charge or transfer money.

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation / remaining validation |
| --- | --- |
| Weak buyer demand | Recruit verified demand; matching cannot create buyers |
| Wrong basket weights | Preserve local units, require measured kg/fixed documented conversion |
| Spoilage uncertainty | Label prototype assumptions; expert inspection; no probability claim |
| Overselling | Locks, constraints, transactions, idempotency; live concurrency acceptance |
| Connectivity | Lightweight forms, optional map downloads, delegated agents; field device tests |
| Transport no-shows | Provider confirmation, deadline incidents, evidence and reasons |
| Privacy breach | Least privilege, private locations/media, audit and response process |
| Broad scope | Prove one crop and full transaction before advanced capabilities |

## International and deferred scope

Keep crops, grades, handling assumptions, local units, currency and timezone explicit. Add real translations and locally validated practices before claiming multilingual support. Review jurisdiction-specific privacy/financial requirements, providers and partner API scopes before expansion.

The PDF explicitly defers live payments, loans, insurance, native mobile apps, IoT, advanced fleet tracking, autonomous execution, sophisticated spoilage ML and global routing optimization. AI does not commit inventory, book services or confirm payments. Interviews, training, calibration and verified field impact cannot be completed by a coding session.
